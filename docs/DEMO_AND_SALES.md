# Demo & Sales Playbook

How to present Unscripted so a studio **understands** it, **accepts** it, and
**buys** a pilot.

## The one sentence

> Unscripted is the model-independent **social-world runtime** that gives existing
> NPC systems causal world-memory, belief with provenance, rumour, factions and
> **explainable** long-term consequences. *The world remembers — and every character
> remembers differently.*

Say what it is **not**, immediately: not a renderer, not a voice engine, not another
chatbot. Inworld/Convai/ACE/MetaHuman sit *beside* it. We never fight on voice, faces
or per-interaction price — those are commoditised.

## Who to show it to

Unreal-based AA RPGs, immersive sims, detective games and social sandboxes with
20–200 named, persistent NPCs (see the studio shortlist in the pitch one-pagers).
The buyer is technical (lead/creative director, systems or AI engineer), and is
usually **already sceptical of LLM NPCs** — that scepticism is our opening, not our
obstacle.

## Open with the map — `unscripted viz`

```bash
unscripted viz --at "4:ask Voss about breach" --out spread.html
```

Ten people, seven rooms, five days, one claim. Watch it leave one man, travel the
station through people who actually meet, arrive in two different versions, and
end with all ten believing it and **not one of them able to recall being told.**

The caption under the map names each moment in a sentence, so the page narrates
itself while you record it. `#frame=120&paused` links straight to the moment
worth showing. Nothing on it is drawn that was not recorded.

This is the thirty seconds that decides whether the rest of the call happens.

## Send this before the call — `unscripted tour`

```bash
unscripted tour --out tour.html --script narration.md
```

Six scenes, generated by running them: repetition that does not become
corroboration, a lie with the liar's name on it, a real language model's blocked
attempts beside what the player actually got, a day of off-screen spread that
still traces to one origin, a liar exposed and the beliefs recomputed, and a
search for the shortest sequence that breaks a stated rule.

One self-contained HTML file — no network, no script tags, opens anywhere. The
narration file is the voiceover for a two-and-a-half minute video, generated from
the same run so the words and the numbers cannot drift apart.

**It sets nothing against an invented competitor**, and says so on every scene.
The comparison is either the model's own recorded output or a named structural
absence. That is not modesty; it is the only version of this page that survives a
technical reader.

## Show, don't tell — the 5-minute demo (use `unscripted demo`)

The UI is the pitch. Drive it live; every claim becomes visible on screen.

1. **"NPCs distinguish truth from hearsay."** Click **Honce**. Ask `ask Honce about clinic`.
   Point at *Subjective knowledge & provenance*: the belief sits at a probability, not a
   fact, and lists **where it came from**. This is the wedge no competitor shows as a product.
2. **"A rumour from one mouth is not three independent facts."** Show the same belief gaining
   a second provenance entry — and that repeated same-origin claims barely move it
   (correlation discount). Lies and contradictory testimony become *gameplay*.
3. **"They feel it, and you can see the face."** The affect panel + live face are driven by
   the same PAD/emotion state — the exact ARKit-52 blendshapes a MetaHuman consumes. One model,
   one source of truth, from reasoning to face.
4. **"Why did it say that?"** Expand **why?** under the NPC's line, and read the **reason trace**
   on the right: belief update → memory encoded → emotion fired → mood shift. This is the
   explainability USP — and the QA argument.
5. **"The world moves off-screen."** `wait 30`, then show **faction heat / clocks** climbing on
   their own. The society develops between conversations, with traceable causes.
6. **"And it's reproducible."** Mention snapshot + deterministic replay: the same run reproduces
   identically — so studios can **test** emergent NPCs instead of fighting them.

End on the line that reframes the whole category: *"We don't replace your authors — we give
them controllable reactivity and continuity they can debug."*

## Handling the three objections

- **"We're sceptical of generative AI in games."** Good — so are we about uncontrolled chatbots.
  Unscripted is deterministic, validated and authored-bounded: secrets provably never leak,
  every output is explainable, and you can run it with **no model at all** (templates) or your
  own local model. We add continuity, not a black box.
- **"We already have Inworld/Convai."** Keep them — for voice and faces. They don't model false
  belief with provenance, correlation-discounted rumour, or an off-screen faction director, and
  they don't give you a reproducible reason trace. We sit above/beside them.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** Nothing per interaction if you want — the core runs locally and
  deterministically. Bring your own model only where you want free language.

## How to package and price

- **Lead with the demo UI**, not slides. Then the reason trace. Then determinism/replay.
- **Sell a pilot, not a licence.** Realistically sellable today: a fixed-scope, fixed-price
  **€25k–60k pilot** — 5–10 NPCs, one scenario, a custom world-pack in *their* genre (not the
  cyberpunk block), text dialogue, and an integration report. The studio keeps the world-pack and
  report regardless. (Full price ladder: `Unscripted_Product_Strategy_*.md` §6.)
- **De-risk integration on the call:** the demo *is* the integration reference — same JSON
  endpoints they would call, plus the MetaHuman avatar packet and engine bridge profiles.

## What exists today (be honest)

- Deterministic, dependency-free runtime; 36 green regression tests.
- Browser demo UI over the real runtime (`unscripted demo`); structured state/reason-trace API.
- Subjective belief + provenance, correlation discount, memory/forgetting, affect, factions/heat,
  validator (provable secret non-leak), snapshot + replay, character generation.
- MetaHuman/Unreal avatar adapter (ARKit-52 + prosody) and Unity/Godot/custom bridges.
- Optional local **GPU** LLM for free NPC text (buffered, cleaned, validated, safe fallback).

Honest status: a strong research prototype, not yet production-hardened. The pilot is exactly how
the narrow core gets hardened against a studio's real content — that framing is a feature, not an
apology.
